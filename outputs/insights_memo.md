# Profit Leakage: Findings Memo

- Sales were ₹4,082,126 with profit of ₹251,996 (6.2% margin). 19% of order lines lost money.
- Orders discounted above 30% earn a -53.7% margin versus 10.8% for the rest. 608 of them lost a combined ₹158,301; capping those discounts could recover up to that amount.
- Loss-making sub-categories: Machines (-₹46,623), Tables (-₹41,517), Bookcases (-₹31,692).
- Weakest region: Central at 3.7% margin; strongest: South at 9.3%.
- The top 20% of customers generate 78% of the profit earned by profitable customers; 157 customers are loss-making in total (-₹72,579).
- Best month: 2023-03 (₹13,150 profit); worst month: 2024-08 (-₹294).
